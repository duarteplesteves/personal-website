# Personal Website

A personal home on the web that presents Duarte through his software work, interests, and reading life.

## Language

**Personal home**:
The website as an enduring expression of its owner, rather than a portfolio optimized primarily for winning work. Professional opportunities are a welcome secondary outcome.
_Avoid_: Portfolio site, résumé site

**Long-term vision**:
The complete direction the personal home may grow toward, including ideas intentionally deferred from its first release.

**Version one**:
The smallest coherent form of the personal home worth publishing, selected from the long-term vision rather than treated as the final site.
_Avoid_: MVP

**Work**:
The umbrella for Duarte’s professional history and selected contributions, presented without implying sole ownership of company projects.
_Avoid_: Portfolio

**Experience**:
A concise account of the companies, roles, responsibilities, and areas in which Duarte worked.
_Avoid_: Résumé

**Selected work**:
Honest stories about specific professional problems, Duarte’s contribution, constraints, and lessons, whether completed or ongoing and only where disclosure is appropriate.
_Avoid_: Personal projects, case studies

**Library**:
The set of books Duarte intentionally tracks, whether owned or not, presented through different views rather than separate book lists.
_Avoid_: Book list, bookshelf

**Collection status**:
Whether a tracked book belongs to Duarte’s physical or digital collection; independent of reading status.
_Avoid_: Library status

**Reading status**:
Duarte’s relationship to reading a tracked book, such as wanting to read it, currently reading it, having read it, or having paused or abandoned it; independent of ownership.
_Avoid_: Collection status

**Equivalent translation**:
Portuguese (Portugal) and English expressions of the same public content that preserve meaning and tone without requiring word-for-word correspondence.
_Avoid_: Literal translation

**Reflection**:
An optional short personal response to a book, distinct from a formal review or required rating.
_Avoid_: Review

**Central thread**:
Duarte’s curiosity about ideas, problems, and possible solutions, expressed through software work, reading, philosophy, and other interests without reducing his identity to his occupation.
_Avoid_: Software-engineer identity, personal brand

**Home**:
The personal home’s main document, revealing its Central thread through a brief introduction, Work, a small Library preview, and direct contact links rather than a long biography.
_Avoid_: About page, landing page

**Standalone section**:
A topic with enough meaningful material and its own browsing need to justify a primary destination. In version one, only the Library qualifies; other topics remain on Home or in detail pages until they earn this status.
_Avoid_: Top-level page
