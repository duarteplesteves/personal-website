# Research restrained visual references

## Question

Which current websites provide useful, complementary references for restrained minimal design, intuitive navigation, subtle interaction delight, bilingual content, or personal-library browsing, and which specific transferable qualities—not wholesale designs—should be considered?

## Answer

The research identified five complementary references and analyzed only their transferable qualities: Emil Kowalski for constrained visual degrees of freedom and purposeful motion; Paco Coursey for a content-led personal home; Canada.ca for same-page bilingual switching semantics; Literal for status-led Library previews; and Derek Sivers for text-first book Reflections. Together they suggest a quiet content column, conventional persistent navigation, counterpart language switching, orthogonal Library views, optional personal voice, and an interaction budget reserved for feedback and rare delight.
