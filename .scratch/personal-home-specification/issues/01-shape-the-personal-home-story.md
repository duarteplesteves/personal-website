# Shape the personal home’s story and release boundaries

## Question

What central story, information hierarchy, and version-one boundary should connect Duarte’s identity, Work, Library, and other interests without turning the site into a résumé or an unfocused collection of pages?

## Answer

The personal home’s Central thread is Duarte’s curiosity about ideas, problems, and possible solutions. Software work, reading, philosophy, and other interests are evidence of that disposition; “software engineer” is an occupation, not the identity around which the site is organized. The site should show this through selected material rather than describe it through a long biography or personal manifesto.

In version one, **Home** is the main personal document: a one- or two-sentence introduction, an Experience timeline with any safe and meaningful Selected Work, a small Library preview whose selection and shape remain to be decided, and direct email, GitHub, and LinkedIn links. Detailed Selected Work is not a launch requirement; concise, honest Experience is enough when disclosure or source material is limited. Other interests may be mentioned naturally but do not receive empty named sections.

The **Library** is the only Standalone section in version one. There is no separate About or Work destination. The header consists of Duarte’s name linking Home, Library, and the language switch. Long term, any topic—including Work, tools/setup, philosophy, or another interest—graduates into a Standalone section only when it has enough meaningful material and an independent browsing need.
