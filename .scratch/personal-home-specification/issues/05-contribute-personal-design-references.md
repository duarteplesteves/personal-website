# Contribute personal design references

## Question

Which additional sites, screenshots, interactions, or other references does Duarte personally respond to, and what specific quality of each should inform—or explicitly not inform—the visual direction?

## Answer

Duarte added Kit Langton, Performance, Guillermo Rauch, and Brian Lovin to the positive reference set. The useful qualities are direct introductions, plainly labelled content groups, generous editorial spacing, scannable text timelines, and the possibility of sharing tools/setup and ways of working. Emil Kowalski and Paco Coursey remain positive references. Canada.ca is retained only for equivalent-page language-switch behavior, while Literal and Derek Sivers should not guide the Library’s visual direction. The version-one Library should inherit the site’s restrained grammar as a simple text-first list without covers; book imagery remains a later option.
